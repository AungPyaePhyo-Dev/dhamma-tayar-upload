mm short

Daily Dhamma talks, published on schedule

mm short is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel ဓမ္မတရားတော်များ to prepare and upload one long Dhamma talk video per day.

ဓမ္မတရားတော်များ YouTube channel အတွက် နေ့စဉ် တရားတော် တစ်ပုဒ်စီ ပြင်ဆင်ပြီး အချိန်ဇယားအတိုင်း တင်ပေးတဲ့ ကိုယ်ပိုင် tool ဖြစ်ပါတယ်။

What it does

  • Turns a recorded Dhamma talk (audio) into a video with still background images.
  • Prepares the title, description and thumbnail for each video.
  • Uploads the video to the owner's own channel as private, with a scheduled publish time.
  • Checks that each upload finished processing, so the same talk is never uploaded twice.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner, on their own computer. mm short is not a public service: there are no sign-ups and no other users. It never accesses anyone else's account.

How it uses Google

The owner signs in once with Google. mm short asks for two permissions:

PermissionWhy
youtube.upload Upload videos and set thumbnails on the owner's channel.
youtube.readonly Read the owner's own upload list and processing status, to avoid duplicates and confirm each video is ready.

It cannot delete videos, read comments, or change channel settings. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
